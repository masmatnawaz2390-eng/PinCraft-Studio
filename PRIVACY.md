## 1. Introduction

PinCraft Studio ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our application, website, and related services.

## 2. What Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information**
- Name, email address, and contact information
- Account credentials and authentication details
- Subscription and billing information

**Content You Create**
- Pin titles, descriptions, keywords, and metadata you generate
- Product information you input for content generation
- Design preferences and content variations
- Notes and editorial decisions

**Communication**
- Messages sent through support channels
- Feedback, bug reports, and feature requests

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Data**
- Features accessed and how you interact with the Service
- Date and time of activities
- Device information (OS, browser type, IP address)
- Performance metrics and error logs

**Cookies and Tracking**
- We use cookies to improve user experience and analytics
- You can control cookie preferences in your browser

## 4. How We Protect Your Information

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption:** Data in transit uses SSL/TLS encryption
- **Access Controls:** Limited employee access to personal data
- **Regular Audits:** Security reviews and vulnerability assessments
- **Secure Storage:** Encrypted databases for sensitive information

However, no security system is impenetrable.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Usage Data:** Retained for 12 months for analytics
- **Deleted Content:** Permanently deleted upon your request
- **Backup Data:** May be retained for 30 days after deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time.

## 7. Your Rights

### GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Users)
You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** your data
- **Restrict** processing
- **Data Portability** – receive your data in portable format
- **Withdraw Consent**
- **Object** to processing

### CCPA Rights (California Users)
You have the right to:
- **Know** what personal data is collected
- **Access** your personal data
- **Delete** your personal data
- **Opt-Out** of data sales

To exercise your rights, email: **privacy@pincraft-studio.dev**

We will respond within 30 days.
