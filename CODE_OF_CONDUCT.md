# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the PinCraft Studio project will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Expected Behavior

✅ Be respectful and inclusive
✅ Welcome different perspectives
✅ Provide constructive feedback
✅ Focus on what's best for the community
✅ Show empathy and patience

## Unacceptable Behavior

❌ Harassment or discrimination
❌ Offensive language or insults
❌ Personal attacks
❌ Trolling or bad faith arguments
❌ Any form of abuse

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to: **conduct@pincraft-studio.dev**

All reports will be reviewed confidentially.

---

Together, we build a better community. Thank you for being part of it.
